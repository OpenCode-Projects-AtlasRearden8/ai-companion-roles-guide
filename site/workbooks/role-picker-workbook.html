Role Picker Workbook

A one-page sieve: turn a vague ask into a role, a job, and a boundary — on paper, before you type it.

Use this workbook when a request keeps coming out mushy. Work top to bottom with a pen. Print a clean copy whenever you want one.

Step 1 — Name the moment

Circle the sentence closest to today's situation:

  • I have a blank page and no starting point.
  • I have words that exist and need work.
  • I know what I want to happen, not how to sequence it.
  • Something confuses me and I want it in plain English.
  • I keep starting and stopping a routine.

Step 2 — Match it to a helper mode

If you circled the blank page, meet the Circle Rule below — the matching mode: Brainstormer.

If you circled words-that-exist: Editor.

If you circled sequencing: Planner.

If you circled confusion: Explainer.

If you circled start-and-stop: Drill Sergeant-Lite.

Step 3 — Write your one-sentence job

One job. One sentence. If it needs "and," split it and pick the first half.

My job for the AI is: _______________________________________________

Step 4 — Choose two boundaries from the Big Four

Mark the two you will actually use today:

  • Length: under ______ words (write the number — numbers beat vibes)
  • Format: as a ______________________________________ (list / table / one message to copy)
  • Tone: like a ______________________________________ (friend at dinner / direct-but-kind coach / your favorite teacher)
  • Scope: do NOT ______________________________________ (recommend products / add tips / interpret my symptoms / go past topic X)

Step 5 — Assemble the opening

Copy your four lines into this box, then paste it into a chat:

ROLE:      You are my __________________________ .
JOB:       ______________________________________________________ .
BOUNDARY:  ______________________________________________________ .
           ______________________________________________________ .

The Circle Rule

When in doubt between two roles, ask:

Am I asking the AI to make MORE things, or BETTER things?

More → Creator family (Brainstormer, Ghost, Naming Committee). Better → Sharpeners (Editor, Clarity Cutter, Plain-Language Translator). If you said "both" — do More first, then rotate. Creation first, criticism second. Always.

Step 6 — The after-check

After the AI answers, put one mark next to the line that broke (or none):

  • Size was off (too long, too short, wrong shape) → tighten the Boundary.
  • Style was off (lectured, flowery, too formal) → name the Tone harder.
  • Honesty was off (agreed with everything, invented a term) → use a save from the manual's "When a Role Breaks" page.

Good work. Keep this page; the second workbook is a two-week routine tracker.
