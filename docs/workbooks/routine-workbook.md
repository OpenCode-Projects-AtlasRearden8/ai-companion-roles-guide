# Drill Sergeant-Lite: 14-Day Routine Workbook

*The printable sidekick to the Drill Sergeant-Lite role: a card you fill in, mark up, and keep on the fridge or clipboard.*

## Part A — Choose your routine

Pick ONE routine. Small beats ambitious; you can add a second routine on day 15.

> My routine: _______________________________________________

> Minutes per day: ______ (under 15 is plenty)

> When it hooks onto my day: _______________________________________________
> (example: "right after my lunch break," "before I clock in")

## Part B — Write the reset clause

This is the part that keeps the drill kind. Copy it onto your card:

> **If I miss a day, I do not catch up and I do not guilt myself.
> I shrink the goal to a 2-minute version and restart the same day.**

## Part D — Two check-in questions to bring to your AI coach

On day 3 and day 10, ask your Drill Sergeant-Lite chat these two things:

1. "What is the smallest version of this routine I could not fail at?"
2. "Based on where I marked circles, what is my most likely drop-off day — and what should I plan for it?"

## Part E — The honest fine print

- This is a habits tool, not a health tool. If low energy, low mood, or a medical issue is making routines hard, that is a conversation for a healthcare professional — not a role card. Education only, always.
- If 14 days fails twice, the routine is wrong-sized, not you. Cut it in half and try again. That answer is allowed; the plan serves you.
